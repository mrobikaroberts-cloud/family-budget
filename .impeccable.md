## Design Context

### Users
The Roberts family — parents and potentially older kids using a shared household budget tracker. Used daily for checking spend vs. budget, reviewing upcoming bills, and getting AI-powered financial guidance. Context: casual home use, desktop browser primary, iPhone and iPad secondary.

### Brand Personality
**Three words**: Playful · Family-forward · Confident  
The app should feel like Apple's Family Sharing met a really good budgeting app — warm, personal, and delightful to open every day. Not a sterile finance tool. Not a kiddy toy. A capable, beautiful tool the whole family is proud to use.

### Aesthetic Direction
**iOS 26 / macOS Tahoe liquid glass** — layered translucent surfaces, tinted blur, specular highlights on card edges, vibrant color moments breaking through glass. Light theme with depth created through glass layers over a subtle mesh gradient background (not flat white). Squircle shapes (generous continuous-curvature corners). Large, expressive typography for hero numbers. Vivid teal accent reading confidently through glass.

Anti-reference: generic AI dashboard (purple-to-blue gradients, glassmorphism as decoration), anything that looks "corporate finance" or "kiddy app."

### Typography
- **Display/headings**: Bricolage Grotesque (variable, opsz) — expressive, warm, strong at large sizes
- **Body/UI**: Figtree — rounded, open, clean without being childish
- Both available on Google Fonts; neither is in the reflex-font banned list

### Design Principles
1. **Glass over flat** — every surface should feel like layered material with depth, not painted flat
2. **Numbers are the heroes** — budget figures deserve display-scale type and color; they're the point
3. **Playful but precise** — rounded forms, warm accents, springy transitions — never clunky or stiff
4. **Mobile-first responsive** — iPhone and iPad layouts matter as much as desktop
5. **Delight in the details** — subtle animations on state changes, satisfying feedback, moments of joy
